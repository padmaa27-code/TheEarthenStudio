Pottery sessions

Come alone. Or don't.

Every session below includes 2 kg of stoneware clay, all tools, an apron, glazing in the colour you choose, and both firings. You throw more than you keep — that is normal, and the ones that collapse are the ones you learn from.

One at the wheel

Two hours, on your own.

Six wheels run at a time with two instructors on the floor, so you are never more than thirty seconds from a hand on your hand. Most people throw four or five pots and keep two.

Two at the wheel

One wheel each, elbows touching.

Two and a half hours, wheels placed side by side, and half an hour at the end with chai while your pots sit on the drying shelf. We photograph the pair before they go in.

The long table

Four to ten people, two stations.

Groups split in half. One half throws, the other hand-builds at the long table, and you swap at the ninety-minute mark. Nobody waits, nobody is bored, and everyone leaves with something. Birthdays, farewells, and one wedding party so far.

Six weeks on the wheel

For the ones who come back.

Six Saturday mornings, the same wheel each week, the same six people. By week four you are throwing to a set weight. By week six you are trimming your own feet and mixing a glaze. Twelve places a term, four terms a year.

₹14,000 for the term · 25 kg clay included · studio access on Wednesdays
