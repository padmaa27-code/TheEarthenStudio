How a booking runs

Five steps and one of them is yours.

The only thing we need from you is a final headcount ten days out. Everything else sits with us.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Tell us headcount, date window and what the day is for. We quote within one working day.

  2. 02

    Hold

    50% and a signed quote holds the date. We block the studio and stop taking public bookings for that slot.

  3. 03

    Headcount

    Final numbers ten working days before. This is when we wedge and portion the clay.

  4. 04

    The session

    You arrive. We have already laid out the wheels with name cards. Nothing for you to set up.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Three weeks later, one crate to your office, every piece wrapped and labelled with its maker's name.
